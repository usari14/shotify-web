3. LONG DESCRIPTION (App Store / About page)
================================================================================

Running a Shopify store means you always need better product images — for ads, PDPs, collections, email, and social. Shotify AI is an all-in-one visual commerce app that lives inside your Shopify admin.

Generate new product photography from your existing catalog images and custom prompts, or use ready-made templates for studio, lifestyle, seasonal, and luxury looks. Review results before anything goes live, then apply images as featured, additional, or replacement media on the product — synced automatically to Shopify Files.

Keep every approved shot in your in-app Library with search by product or prompt. When you are ready to go social, connect your Instagram Professional account and publish from the library with captions.

Engage shoppers on-site with Shoppable Video widgets: upload image or video slides, link products, publish to Shopify metaobjects, and drop a theme block on any page. Track widget engagement with storefront analytics.

Flexible plans and credit-based generation (via Mantle billing) scale with your catalog size. Whether you are launching SKUs weekly or refreshing a seasonal collection, Shotify AI is built to move creative work out of Photoshop and into your daily workflow.

6. BEST USAGE — RECOMMENDED WORKFLOWS
================================================================================

--- WORKFLOW A: Quick win for a single SKU ---
1. Install app and choose a plan.
2. Dashboard → Select a Product (or Products page).
3. Pick up to 6 reference images from the product gallery.
4. Enter a short prompt OR go to Templates for a one-click style.
5. Generate → review 1–4 results → Apply as featured or additional image.
6. Image appears in Library and on the live product in Shopify.

Best for: new launches, hero image refresh, A/B testing a new look.

--- WORKFLOW B: Batch refresh with templates ---
1. Browse Templates → pick a style (e.g. Studio White).
2. Select product → job queues in background.
3. Open Jobs (or wait for redirect) until status is complete.
4. Review on generate page with ?jobId= → apply best shot.
5. Repeat for top sellers in your catalog.

Best for: seasonal campaigns, collection-wide visual consistency.

--- WORKFLOW C: Social + onsite together ---
1. Generate and apply strong product images (Workflow A or B).
2. Connections → Connect Instagram (Professional account).
3. Library → open image → compose caption → Publish to Instagram.
4. Shoppable Videos → create widget with product-linked video slides.
5. Online Store → Theme Editor → add Shoppable Videos block → select widget handle.
6. Monitor Analytics for generation usage and widget performance.

Best for: brands running paid social + onsite conversion from the same assets.

--- WORKFLOW D: Shoppable video only ---
1. Shoppable Videos → New widget.
2. Upload cover + slide media (video or image); link each slide to a product.
3. Save & Publish → confirm metaobject sync.
4. Theme → add block to homepage or collection template.
5. Toggle widget enabled/disabled from list view without republishing theme code.

Best for: fashion, beauty, electronics — categories where video drives conversion.

9. SHOPIFY THEME / STOREFRONT SETUP (shoppable video)
================================================================================

Short instructions for your docs/FAQ page:

1. In Shotify AI, create and publish a Shoppable Widget (status: published, sync complete).
2. In Shopify Admin → Online Store → Themes → Customize.
3. Add the "Shoppable Videos" app block to the desired template/section.
4. Enter the widget handle (from the app widget list).
5. Adjust layout: carousel vs cards, autoplay, arrows, aspect ratio, CTA text ("Visit Product").
6. Save theme. Widget reads live data from Shopify metaobjects — no manual JSON editing.

13. FAQ (website / support)
================================================================================

Q: Do I need professional photos before using Shotify?
A: You need at least one product image from Shopify to use as reference. More references (up to 6) usually improve consistency.

Q: Will AI images replace my photos automatically?
A: No. You review results and choose how to apply them (featured, add, or replace).

Q: What image styles are included?
A: Eight+ templates across Studio, Lifestyle, Brand, and Seasonal categories, plus fully custom prompts.

Q: How do credits work?
A: Templates show credit cost (most use 1 credit; premium styles may use more). Subscribe via Plans; generation consumes credits per Mantle billing.

Q: Can I use Shotify without Instagram?
A: Yes. Photo generation, library, and shoppable video work independently. Instagram is optional for publishing.

Q: What account do I need for Instagram?
A: Instagram Professional (Business or Creator). Personal accounts are not supported for the Graph API publishing flow.

Q: How do shoppable videos appear on my store?
A: Publish a widget in the app, then add the Shoppable Videos theme block and select your widget handle.

Q: Is my data shop-specific?
A: Yes. Connections, generations, widgets, and library entries are scoped to your Shopify shop.

Q: Can I disconnect Instagram without logging in again?
A: Yes. Use the Disconnect button on Connections. Reconnect opens OAuth only when you choose Reconnect.
